# ⚡ Dimensional Flux

### *Wireless Forge Energy across dimensions.*

![Dimensional Flux — wireless Forge Energy across dimensions](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/f2f58fac7ec7b60e1a630612d2ca0a8808e64d16.png)

**Dimensional Flux** sends Forge Energy (FE) **without a single cable**. Put an Emitter on a generator, a Receiver on a machine, link both to the same network and the energy arrives, in the same base or in another dimension.

Every network has a name, a color, its own members and full statistics. Around it: the **Dimensional Battery**, the **Dimensional Controller**, the **Tesla Broadcaster**, the **Dimensional Configurator** and an animated **Dimensional Flux Guide** that explains everything in game.

## 🔌 Dimensional Emitter

![Dimensional Emitter](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/2f99bd5baf9e966f58ced88223bff918e8397489.png)

The way in. Mount it on any face of a generator or battery and it sends that energy into your network.

- ⚡ Generators that push energy fill it on their own; passive stores are emptied as the network needs them.
- 🔢 Priority, transfer limit and redstone modes.
- 🎨 Glows in your network color.

> ⚛️ **Need energy to feed your network?** Build a reactor or a turbine from **[Bigger Reactors Rework](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biggerreactors-rework)** and put an Emitter on it: one generator, power for the whole network.

## 📡 Dimensional Receiver

![Dimensional Receiver](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/16e7f6db8224dde0917ae8346d55373bbe933caa.png)

The way out. Mount it on a machine and it hands over exactly what the machine asks for, from any dimension.

- 📶 Its antenna moves while energy flows.
- 🔁 A backup network takes over when the main one runs dry.
- 🔢 Priority and FE/t limit per device: the higher number is served first.

> ⛏️ **Need resources?** The **[Dimensional Flux Miner](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/dimensional-flux-miner)** makes the ores of its dimension appear out of nothing. Its Crusher and Electric Furnace love a Receiver, and the Miner can even produce Dimensional Flux Ore.

## 🔋 Dimensional Battery

![Dimensional Battery](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/4901c95617a8af48f8d804f08f8c60449016d14d.png)

Stores what the network does not use now and gives it back when it runs short, always after the machines.

- 🔋 **256,000,000 FE** of capacity and **720,000 FE/t** of transfer (configurable).
- 📶 An eight-bar gauge on the block and a comparator output.
- 🧰 A broken battery keeps its charge.

## 🧠 Dimensional Controller

![Dimensional Controller](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/e81af5b0a4144af889b081c0d74af81ae5cd17bd.png)

The heart of a network: wireless charging across dimensions and the statistics of the whole network.

- 🧲 Charges armor, hands, hot bar and Curios slots, with a total FE/t limit.
- 📊 Live input and output, top consumers and generators, every connection.
- ⚠️ Deficit alerts before the reserve runs out.

## 🌩️ Tesla Broadcaster

![Tesla Broadcaster](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/a4010c86740dcae1eca48192d2878ae6c72c73f6.png)

Powers every machine inside its area, with no cables and no connectors.

- 📐 Area from 9 to 32 blocks, offset on each axis.
- 🧊 Live 3D preview of the covered machines.
- ✅ Turn each machine on or off, rename it, give it its own priority and limit.
- ⚡ Electric arcs while it works.

## 🪄 Dimensional Configurator

![Dimensional Configurator](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/1641f6fc4e95402873c3264401214cb6d445fb91.png)

Copies the network and settings of one device and pastes them on another: perfect for a wall of Receivers.

- 🖱️ Sneak + use to copy, use to paste, sneak in the air to clear.

- 👁️ Shows hidden devices nearby.

## 🖥️ Screens

Every device opens a screen with side tabs; each tab opens a window you can move, and several stay open together.

![Device screen and its settings](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/a8bfd23a1fed3174ca9d731b8a83e3e917a37443.png)

- 🌗 **Light or dark look**, particles on or off, for one device or all of them.
- 🔴 **Redstone tab**: always on, only with a signal or only without one.
- 🗺️ **Load Chunk** keeps a far generator working.

![Choosing and creating networks](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/ffb8d076dcb632874a2873cc1da8d85243c434e8.png)

- 🔐 **Public**, **Private** or **Locked** with a password.
- 🔁 Right click a network to make it the **backup**.

![Statistics and connections](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/267ae33b0816b9145f8411deebf70a8d04933d93.png)

- 📈 Input in green, output in red, the balance and the storage gauge.
- 🔦 Light any machine up through the walls from the connections list.

![Members and network options](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/e5e302ae9673bcebdb653b6ab859b11cc7cb0327.png)

- 👥 Users, administrators and blocks; share with your FTB Teams party or your `/team`.
- ⚖️ **Distribution**: serve by priority, or share what is left in proportion to what each machine asks for.

![Charging items](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/fbed8346538cc39c484a3216e87d12ae275d4cfc.png)

- 🧲 Choose which slots charge and the total FE/t they may take.

![The dark look](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/ufMZiRnE/images/1657630667de837944037d668c1d1f225045775f.png)

## ❓ FAQ

**Does it generate energy?**
No. Bring your generators from any other mod, such as [Bigger Reactors Rework](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/biggerreactors-rework).

**Does it need a specific energy mod?**
No. Any Forge Energy (FE) source and machine works.

**Does it work across dimensions?**
Yes. A network reaches every dimension, as long as the devices' chunks are loaded (Load Chunk helps).

**Can I use it in a modpack?**
Yes.
